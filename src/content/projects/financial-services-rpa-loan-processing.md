problem: "Manual loan application processing took 8-10 days and involved 6 different teams. Error rates were 3-4% due to manual data entry and workflow miscommunication. The backlog was growing faster than capacity."
approach: "Built an end-to-end RPA solution using UiPath with Power Automate orchestration. outcome: "Processing time reduced from 8 days to 48 hours. Error rate dropped to 0.3%. System now handles 400+ applications per month vs. 250 previously, using the same human team. Compliance documentation is automatically generated. The firm estimates 18-month ROI of 280%."
